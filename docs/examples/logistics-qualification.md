# Partner Readiness Lab Scenario Pack

Pack version: 1
Scenario: qualification
Track: seller
Focus: medd_metrics
Mode: guided

## Target company

Company: Example Logistics
Industry: Logistics

Example Logistics is a fictional regional carrier coordinating deliveries across 18 distribution sites.

Operations teams handle route exceptions across phone, email, and spreadsheet handoffs. Vendors, integration details, financial baselines, budget, and decision process are unknown.

The company wants fewer manual status updates without reducing human control over sensitive customer commitments.

Likely stakeholders include regional operations, dispatch, IT, security, finance, and procurement. Their authority and requirements need discovery.

## Buyer persona

Name: Avery Singh
Role: SVP Operations

Avery owns service reliability and regional operating performance.

Priorities include fewer manual exception handoffs, consistent customer updates, and clear operating ownership.

Concerns include unproven value, disruption during peak periods, integration effort, and unclear accountability for automated responses.

Avery can sponsor discovery but budget authority and final approval are unknown.

## Product or service

Route-exception workflow automation with human approval for sensitive customer commitments.

## Practice objective

Establish the current exception workflow and baseline, identify the operational owner and missing decision evidence, and earn a bounded validation session.
