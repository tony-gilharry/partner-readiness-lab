# Scenario recording guide

Record only after you connect the relevant public agents and complete the live verification checklist in `agent/README.md`. Do not use a mocked session, accelerated timer, or local rule match as evidence of live ElevenLabs behavior.

## Core seller recording, about 2 minutes

**0:00 to 0:20, readiness context**

Show Northstar, Maya Chen, Full discovery, the seller track, and Guided review.

"This is a readiness rehearsal for an external partner seller. Northstar and its executives are fictional. The seller needs to establish the customer problem, connect the offer to customer and partner value, and earn a concrete next step."

**0:20 to 0:40, executive and framework selection**

Switch among Maya, Daniel Okafor, and Priya Raman, then show a MEDDPICC or Command of the Message focus.

"Each executive has different priorities, coaching, evidence, and a separately configured agent voice. Frameworks are optional practice lenses, not a script or a linear stage sequence."

**0:40 to 1:15, verified live exchange**

Show a short real WebRTC excerpt with transcript, script-ready shortcut, one verbatim response, the buyer challenging an unsupported claim, and the learner correcting it. Show that End conversation remains available and audio stops.

"The official SDK supplies the live session and real conversation ID. Local rules select one role-specific script. There is no second AI coach and nothing auto-sends or auto-speaks."

**1:15 to 1:35, evidence and session behavior**

Show the listening estimate, selected focus evidence, buyer-specific evidence, handoff cue, and possible buying signal.

"Local rules make coaching inspectable. The listening estimate uses volume samples, not transcript words. Possible signals are not purchase intent."

**1:35 to 1:55, native evidence and human review**

Show actual ElevenLabs history for that conversation, then the scenario rubric and human confirmation.

"Native evaluation stays in ElevenLabs. The human reviewer compares available evidence with the selected scenario and buyer outcomes. Opening history alone verifies nothing."

**1:55 to 2:00, close**

Show the prototype print record with account, buyer, scenario, real conversation ID, duration, listening estimate, evidence, and disclaimer.

## Custom account recording, about 60 seconds

Show a fictional target-company Markdown file and separate buyer-persona file. Paste or upload them, enter a product/service and objective, and export the plain-Markdown scenario pack.

"Custom practice combines a target company, buyer, and offer. Files are read locally as inert text and stored in this browser. Bounded context goes to ElevenLabs only when a live session starts. Northstar facts do not leak into custom sessions."

Import the exported pack, configure the generic custom-buyer agent, and show a verified live excerpt. Demonstrate an unsupported file rejection if useful.

## Technical and Free-practice recording, about 45 seconds

Show Technical validation and handoff with Daniel or Priya, followed by a verified specialist-handoff moment and technical validation close.

Then show Free practice.

"Free practice keeps voice, transcript, timer, and coaching but removes evidence scoring, evaluation gates, human review, and completion claims."

## Sample imperfect moments

Seller: "This will cut your support cost by 40 percent, and my partner team can guarantee a higher billable rate." The buyer should challenge both claims. The learner should retract the guarantees and propose baseline discovery and sourced validation.

Solutions consultant: "We can connect directly to your existing telephony and confirm production readiness today." Daniel or Priya should challenge the assumed vendor and commitment. The learner should map unknown architecture or security obligations and propose a specialist-supported validation session.

## Claims boundary

Use only real captured IDs, metrics, native results, voices, and review decisions. Do not speed up the timer to claim a live session. Do not recreate native results in the portal. Keep independent-project and fictional or non-confidential-context disclaimers visible.
